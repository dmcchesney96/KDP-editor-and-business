## Product and cover

Title: ABC of the Bible

Subtitle: A Colorful Learn-and-Play Activity Book for Ages 2–5

80-page, adult-supported A–Z activity paperback; 8.5 × 11 inches, standard color on white paper, no interior bleed. Dotted crayon letter practice, counting, matching/circling, coloring, garden tracing/drawing, original short Bible connections, and grown-up prompts. Younger toddlers can point and make marks; tracing is readiness-led. Avoid claims of independent toddler handwriting, spiral/lay-flat binding, single-sided pages, markers, wipe-clean reuse, or guaranteed outcomes.
