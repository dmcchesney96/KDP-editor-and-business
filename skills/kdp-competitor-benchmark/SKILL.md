# KDP Competitor Benchmark

## Job

Own the bounded transformation:

`COMPARABLE BOOKS / SEARCH RESULTS → COMPETITOR BENCHMARK`

## Comparable-set rule

Prefer true reader substitutes over famous books that only share a broad category.

Separate:

- direct comparables;
- adjacent comparables;
- aspirational references.

## Capture

When available, capture:

- title/subtitle;
- author/brand;
- format;
- price;
- page count;
- trim/color if visible;
- publication/revision recency;
- review count;
- rating;
- BSR/category rank;
- cover pattern;
- promise/value proposition;
- interior/activity pattern when previewable;
- recurring complaints/praise from reviews;
- differentiation cues.

## Rank and sales

BSR is an observed Amazon rank signal at a point in time.

Any conversion from BSR to daily/monthly sales is a `TOOL_ESTIMATE`.

Use ranges and explain uncertainty. Do not imply Amazon publishes an official BSR-to-sales formula.

## Analysis

Identify:

- table stakes;
- dominant conventions;
- quality bar;
- price band;
- review moat;
- weak/common complaints;
- over-served patterns;
- under-served reader jobs;
- meaningful white space.

Do not assume a low-review book is easy to beat; it may simply have low demand.

## Longitudinal mode

If multiple snapshots exist, compare:

- BSR movement;
- review growth;
- price changes;
- new editions;
- new competitors;
- persistence vs one-time spikes.

Prefer trends over a single snapshot when the decision is consequential.

## Output

### COMP SET
Direct vs adjacent comparables.

### MARKET TABLE
A concise comparison of the most useful fields.

### TABLE STAKES
What readers appear to expect.

### COMPETITIVE BARRIERS
What makes entry difficult.

### OPENINGS
What a better/different book could do.

### ESTIMATE CAVEATS
Any modeled/uncertain values.

### IMPLICATION FOR OUR BOOK
Concrete design and positioning consequences.
