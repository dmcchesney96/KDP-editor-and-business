# KDP Opportunity Decision Brief

## Job

Own the bounded transformation:

`RESEARCH PACKET → ACTIONABLE PUBLISHING DECISION`

Do not redo all research unless the packet has a critical gap.

## Decision dimensions

Score qualitatively:

- reader need/value;
- evidence of demand;
- competition;
- differentiation;
- product quality potential;
- production complexity;
- policy/IP risk;
- discoverability;
- economics where known;
- strategic fit;
- uncertainty.

## Decision

Choose one:

- `PROCEED` — enough evidence and fit to build.
- `PROTOTYPE / TEST` — promising but one or more major assumptions need validation.
- `REVISE POSITIONING` — the book may work, but audience/promise/format/metadata needs redesign.
- `HOLD` — insufficient evidence or timing.
- `STOP` — weak reader value, poor fit, excessive risk, or unattractive opportunity.

## Output

### DECISION
One label plus one sentence.

### WHY
3–6 decisive reasons.

### WHAT WE KNOW
Only observed/verified evidence.

### WHAT IS ESTIMATED
BSR sales estimates, inferred demand, or heuristics.

### BIGGEST RISK
One primary failure mode.

### REQUIRED DIFFERENTIATION
What the book must do better/differently.

### NEXT ACTION
The smallest concrete action that advances the project.
