# KDP Category Positioning

## Job
Own: `FINISHED BOOK + MARKET CONTEXT + CURRENT KDP SELECTOR → CATEGORY RECOMMENDATION`

## Authority
Category names and availability can change by marketplace and format. Verify the current KDP category selector before final submission.

## Method
1. Start from the actual finished content and intended reader.
2. Inspect categories used by close comparables where observable.
3. Identify accurate browse contexts: topic, audience, activity/format, learning goal, faith/topic where applicable.
4. Compare category relevance and competitive context.
5. Recommend up to the number currently allowed by KDP, subject to live selector availability.
6. Distinguish `CANDIDATE` from `VERIFIED-IN-LIVE-KDP`.

## Guardrails
- Accuracy outranks low competition.
- Do not choose tangential categories merely to chase a bestseller badge.
- Do not infer eligibility for age/grade categories the book does not truthfully serve.
- Recheck categories after format changes.
