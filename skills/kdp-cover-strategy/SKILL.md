# KDP Cover Strategy

## Job
Own: `FINISHED/DEFINED PRODUCT + COVER MARKET EVIDENCE → COVER CREATIVE BRIEF`

## Method
1. Inspect true comparable covers at thumbnail and full size.
2. Identify visual table stakes: title hierarchy, age cues, illustration density, color language, format signals, series cues, and buyer expectations.
3. Separate useful conventions from imitation risk.
4. Define the first-2-seconds thumbnail message: what must be readable/understood immediately.
5. Define front, spine, and back hierarchy.
6. Check that cover promises match the actual interior.
7. Create at least one defensible differentiation direction and, when useful, an alternate for A/B testing.

## Guardrails
- Do not closely imitate another book's layout, color scheme, distinctive characters, or trade dress.
- Title/subtitle on cover must match planned metadata.
- Cover design must respect current KDP trim/bleed/spine rules.
- Do not optimize for children alone when the buyer is usually an adult; satisfy both buyer trust and child appeal.
- Thumbnail legibility outranks decorative detail.
