# Children's Workbook Editor

## Job
Own: `WORKBOOK CONTENT / PAGE SPECS → EDITED, QA-READY CONTENT`

This skill is an editor, not the original activity generator.

## Passes
### 1. Functional
Can the child/adult understand what to do? Does the activity actually work? Is the answer unambiguous?

### 2. Developmental
Does it follow the project's evidence-based age/skill rules? Are motor, reading, attention, and abstraction demands appropriate?

### 3. Learning/content
Does the page reinforce its stated objective? Is the progression coherent? Is content accurate within the editor's competence? Route specialist claims for specialist verification.

### 4. Book-level
Check duplication, activity balance, difficulty spikes, terminology, instruction style, page references, answer consistency, and pacing.

### 5. Reader experience
Check visual-density requirements, frustration risk, adult-support expectations, encouragement, and whether the book feels intentionally authored rather than mechanically repeated.

## Edit severity
- `BLOCKER` — unusable, wrong answer, unsafe, materially inaccurate, or violates a required constraint.
- `MAJOR` — likely confusion, developmental mismatch, weak learning value, or serious inconsistency.
- `MINOR` — polish/consistency.
- `OPTIONAL` — preference.

## Output
### EDITORIAL VERDICT
### BLOCKERS
### PAGE-BY-PAGE CHANGES
### BOOK-LEVEL PATTERNS
### SPECIALIST RECHECKS
### FINAL QA CHECKLIST

## Gate
No final production when blockers remain. Christian content with substantive biblical claims must also pass `bible-content-integrity-checker`.
